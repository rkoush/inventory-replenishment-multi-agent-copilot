# Supplier Contract Summary (FY2026)

Owner: Procurement. Summary of key ordering terms; the system of record is the supplier table.

## DairyFresh Co (GRO-001 Whole Milk, GRO-002 Large Eggs)

Lead time is 2 days. Milk ships in cases of 4 with a minimum order of 24 units.
Eggs ship in cases of 15 with a minimum order of 30 units. Orders are delivered daily except Sunday.

## Sunrise Bakery (GRO-003 Sandwich Bread)

Lead time is 1 day. Bread ships in trays of 12 with a minimum order of 24 units.

## FreshFields Produce (GRO-004 Bananas)

Lead time is 3 days. Bananas ship in cases of 20 bags with a minimum order of 40 bags.

## CleanHome Supply (HH-001 Paper Towels, HH-002 Laundry Detergent, HH-003 Dish Soap)

Lead time is 5 days. Case packs: paper towels 6, detergent 4, dish soap 12.
Minimum orders: paper towels 24, detergent 16, dish soap 24 units.

## BrightSmile Brands (PC-001 Toothpaste, PC-002 Shampoo)

Lead time is 7 days. Toothpaste ships in cases of 12 (minimum 24); shampoo in cases of 6 (minimum 12).

## VoltMax Distribution (ELE-001 AA Batteries)

Lead time is 10 days. Batteries ship in cases of 10 with a minimum order of 10 units.
