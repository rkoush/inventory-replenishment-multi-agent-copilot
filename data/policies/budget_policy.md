# Inventory Budget Policy (FIN-INV-07)

Owner: Store Finance.

## Weekly purchasing budget

Each store has a weekly replenishment budget cap of $5,000. Requests above the cap require
regional finance approval and must not be submitted automatically by planning tools.

## Concentration limit

No single SKU may consume more than 30% of the weekly replenishment budget. This protects
against one large order crowding out the rest of the assortment.

## Approval

Every purchase order generated by a planning system must be reviewed and approved by the
store manager or an inventory planner before it is sent to a supplier.
