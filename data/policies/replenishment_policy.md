# Store Replenishment Policy (RP-2026-03)

Owner: Merchandising Operations. Applies to all stores in the pilot region (S001, S002).

## Planning horizon and order-up-to rule

Replenishment is planned weekly. The planning horizon is 7 days. Each order must cover
expected demand for the supplier lead time plus the 7-day planning horizon, plus safety stock.
Planners must not order more than the shelf capacity can hold at the time the delivery arrives.

## Safety stock by category

Safety stock is expressed in days of average forecast demand:

- Grocery: 3 days of safety stock (perishable, short shelf life, frequent deliveries).
- Household: 7 days of safety stock.
- Personal Care: 7 days of safety stock.
- Electronics: 10 days of safety stock (long lead times, high stockout cost).

## Stockouts and service level

Grocery staples (milk, eggs, bread, bananas) are service-critical. If the budget does not
allow every SKU to reach its target, planners should prioritize closing the gap on items
whose shortage would cost the most in lost sales.
